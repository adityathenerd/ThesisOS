## Decision and minimum boundary

**Is an introductory conversation worth pursuing?** Recommend an outreach candidate when (a) the exact company/project identity and mandate scope are sufficiently resolved to know whom/what the conversation concerns, (b) a concrete product, program or problem can be described from sources, and (c) a credible source-supported reason makes an introductory discussion useful. Do not require proof sufficient for a final investment decision.

A private demo, detailed current contribution, asset rights/access, validation reports and clinical/scientific uncertainty normally become explicit conversation questions. They are not universal pre-intro blockers. A positive category is permission to recommend a human-reviewed introduction opportunity, not proof of claims, accepted qualification, a complete outreach package, or permission to send.

| Conversation category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| outreach_candidate | The three minimum conditions above hold and no material known contrary fact makes the proposed conversation misleading or outside scope. Carry doubts into the call agenda. |
| research_first | A specific identity/scope/current-status contradiction or missing concrete rationale prevents an honest useful introduction. Name the one fact to resolve and what changes afterward; do not use this as a bucket for every diligence gap. |
| monitor | A specific future event would create conversation merit that does not exist yet. A budget pause or missing private report is not itself that event. |
| outside_scope | Affirmative evidence establishes a mandate mismatch or an inappropriate target. Missing publicity/coverage is not rejection. |

No category quotas, numerical scores, pedigree requirements, new geography/revenue/headcount restrictions, or forced positives. Early programs and functional tools can warrant introductory conversations before private demonstrations or complete legal/scientific diligence. Founder quality and current contactability must not be inferred from search visibility.

## Separate contact actionability

Record conversation merit separately from **relevant person status** (source-attributed current / historical or uncertain / unknown) and **contact-route status** (existing source-supported route / route not established / human confirmation needed). An otherwise credible project may be an outreach_candidate while its named builder/route still needs resolution. Never invent a founder, address, role or currentness. A generic company route is not a verified personal route.

## 3. Required pursue-to-reassessment loop

1. Restate the saved decisive question and bind it to current case/evidence/assessment versions.
2. Select one highest-value check below. Confirm the route exists and is authorized; backend's capability check is authoritative. Public-source research owners need an identity/URL assignment before fetching to avoid duplication.
3. Execute that check within the authorized allowance. Save attempted source/request, time, outcome, evidence/attribution and limits. An unavailable capability is a recorded inability to attempt, not a failed company fact.
4. If material evidence arrives, accept it through the owned evidence path, rebuild the context and reassess. Preserve the previous recommendation and explain what changed. Do not count a second reading of identical text as independent corroboration.
5. If unresolved, stop the current investigation with the actual attempts and human research questions. State exactly what answer/artifact would permit reassessment. Do not repeat pursue or trigger another paid call merely to restate the gap.
6. Continue only for a newly justified, permitted check or an explicit human continuation. Otherwise wait for the named answer, expert review or genuine future event. Qualification and outreach remain separate human-governed actions.

## 4. Capability routing: use each source for what it can answer

**P — Provider profiles/posts/affiliations, only if capability and scope are verified:** Potentially help identify a person, source a current/historical role, locate dated substantive work or an actual professional participation record. Current batch Company Search proves none of these additional fields are available. Missing posts or profile fields are unknown, not low grit. Provider data cannot by itself demonstrate a product, verify a license, prove an experiment, or establish clinical utility.

**A — Public artifact, experiment, study or registry:** Worked input/output, current docs and release, original methods/results/contribution statement, intended-use validation, sponsor-reported study status. Repository license and publication access are not proof of commercial company rights. A trial registration or completed status is not a published successful result.

**H — Human/private material or specialist judgment:** Authorized demonstration, unrelated customer corroboration, private experiment report, license/rights agreement, sample/model access, or clinical/scientific interpretation. Prepare questions for the human; do not automatically contact founders/customers or access private data.

## 6. Founder ecosystem graph: meaningful ties only

Store a professional tie as a dated, sourced relationship with a specific participation/work fact and limits. Useful examples: coauthored a named paper with a contribution statement; built a particular tool at an institution; worked on a documented company project; participated in a named accelerator/clinical partnership; or published dated technical work connected to the product/program. Attach event precision, observation date, claim origin, role and whether the fact is source-reported or inferred.

Coauthorship supports a shared publication, not friendship, cofounder chemistry or sole invention. Overlapping employers support possible proximity, not that two people worked together. Following, likes, prestige, investor logos and follower count are not contribution or grit scores. Missing public activity is unknown. A new ecosystem edge should either improve entity resolution, explain relevant contribution/access, or change a concrete research question; otherwise defer it.

Replicate, SlideScope and Find Nuclei first need reliable named-person linkage. Existing named teams need contribution-specific enrichment rather than generic biography volume. NeoTargets already has a strong public collaboration/contribution route; Aphotic's academic artifact needs technical/rights mapping; Cadex needs current rather than historical roles. No need to enrich every available person before doing the decisive product/science check.

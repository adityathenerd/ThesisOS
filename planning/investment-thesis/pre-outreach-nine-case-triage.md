## Existing-owner seam

The JSON contains category/rationale, three minimum-basis findings with evidence IDs, up to two sourced call questions, contrary facts, risks, an explicit decisive blocker where applicable, exact input_ref/evidence hash, original assessment_ref, snapshot path/hash, and separate source-attributed person/contact states. Backend/agents may map this review guidance additively under their authorized work. Raw overlay/guidance hashes must be pinned, and current case/evidence binding checked before display or acceptance. Changes to the old C4, strict partner readiness, human handoffs or qualification are not supplied by this overlay.

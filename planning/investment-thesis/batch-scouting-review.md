| Track | Stronger initial signal | Next question that changes the case | Common false positive |
|---|---|---|---|
| H | Named workflow, identifiable operational user, specific functionality and attributable use claim | Does the reusable product actually replace work after integration and review overhead? | Marketing agency or process consultancy describing healthcare clients as a platform |

Missing traction, incomplete clinical evidence at an appropriate early stage, and absent public IP documents remain unknown rather than automatic negatives.

## Founder evidence that warrants attention

- **Identity and role:** A company-linked source identifies the person and role. Preserve founder, CEO, CTO, employee, author, advisor, and investor as different roles. A CEO title is not proof of founder status; the founding CTO of a previous employer is not necessarily founder of the present company.
- **Relevant contribution:** A source attributes a specific product, experiment, technical decision or program to the person. Paper authorship is a lead, not proof of invention, ownership, or a company role. Where available, inspect contribution statements and a second appropriately scoped source.
- **Problem proximity:** The person's experience connects to the actual workflow or biological problem, with a concrete observation that shaped the approach. An employer or degree without that link is weak evidence of founder advantage.
- **Learning over time:** Dated changes, experiments or deployments show what the team learned and changed. One launch cannot establish sustained velocity. Do not infer grit, resilience, tenure, compatibility, or exceptional ability from biographies.
- **Control/access:** Relevant access to users, samples, facilities, core technology or rights is evidenced or specifically unresolved. Outsourced experiments are not disqualifying; investigate who designs, interprets and can repeat the work.

There are no numerical pedigree/grit scores, mandatory degrees, founder-count limits, or new geography/revenue/headcount gates. Record the exact support and the limit of the conclusion. If only a company page is available, say company-reported rather than independently verified.

## Concise company/founder brief for frontend and reviewer

Lead with the useful answer, then evidence and uncertainty. Each brief should make the following intelligible without opening a graph:

1. **What it does:** “[Company] builds [specific product/program] for [user/population], addressing [bottleneck] through [mechanism].” If that sentence cannot be supported, state exactly what description is unresolved.
2. **Who is doing it:** Named people, their source-supported current roles and the particular contribution relevant to the case. Explicitly label unknown founder status or historical tenure.
3. **Why investigate:** One concrete thesis link and its strongest source-backed signal, with date and evidence mode. Funding alone is not product or scientific proof.
4. **Why hold/pass:** The strongest contrary fact or material gap. “No evidence found” is a research outcome, not proof that no product exists.
5. **Next action:** One prioritized check, why it matters, and how the plausible answers change the disposition. Give why-now as unknown when no substantive catalyst is established.

Display company facts, source claims and model interpretation distinctly. An accessible source is not automatically independent corroboration. Do not label every unqualified case Monitor: require a useful observable trigger; a poor discovery match may simply be a documented pass or deprioritized lead under the existing policy.

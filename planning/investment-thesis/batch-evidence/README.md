## Per-company deliverable

Save a readable `<candidate-id>-evidence.md` and, when useful, the parallel JSON using evidence-template.json. Copy the exact returned entity identity and observed batch selection reference. Return the concrete facts needed for the five-part brief, rather than a full new company dossier.

1. Company/product/program in one sentence; actual user or indication; proposed primary/secondary track.
2. Named founder/operator, current source-attributed role and exact contribution; unknown founder status/tenure remains unknown.
3. Two or three decisive product/experimental observations, each with URL, retrieval time, publication/event date if visible, attribution, source group, evidence mode, and limitations.
4. What the observation supports under C1–C8 and what it does not establish; include conflicting evidence and failed fetches where material.
5. One prioritized follow-up and the possible decision change. Do not mark partner-review readiness or qualification from an unexamined abstract/title/press release.

For scientific results preserve task/indication, model or population, input/sample context, comparator/controls, measured endpoint, maturity, and whether underlying methods/data were inspected. Preclinical findings, predictions and human findings are distinct. Do not invent absent study details.

## Source grouping and quotation rules

Company homepage, team page, and announcement are normally one company-origin group. Republishing the announcement on a newswire does not create independent corroboration. A company-authored paper has a different evidence form but may share claim origin; record that dependency. A named investor's release establishes the investor's statement, not independent scientific validation.

Prefer concise paraphrases. If a short exact quote materially helps, retain no more than 25 words total from a single non-lyrical source across the sidecar, name the speaker/author, and mark the quotation as exact. Store only the excerpt needed to support the claim, not full source text. Research summaries must respect source word limits as well.

Retrieval timestamps reflect an actual successful or failed attempt. Access date is not publication date. Never label a previously viewed source freshly retrieved if the current attempt failed. Never use an unavailable page as though it was read.

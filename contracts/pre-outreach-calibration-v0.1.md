## Meaning of a lighter threshold

Require a resolved company/project identity and scope; a concrete sourced problem/product/program; and a credible, attributable reason that a short conversation could advance the mandate. Allow company-reported claims at their actual source strength. Strong proof is a reason to prioritize, not an absolute prerequisite for every introductory conversation. Keep actual material contrary evidence visible and distinguish it from missing data.

Private product demonstrations, detailed implementation ownership, current responsibilities, customer corroboration, commercial rights/access, experimental reports and clinical validity can become discovery questions when asking the team is the useful next step. Do not silently treat those facts as resolved. A computational result remains computational, a planned talk remains planned, an indexed role remains source-reported, and clinical validity is never inferred from a scouting classification.

Separate **conversation merit** from **contact preparation**: a relevant person may already be identified, may need current-role confirmation, or may still need identifying. An interesting company with an unknown builder is not automatically a bad prospect; an outreach recommendation is not a claim that a usable contact or sending authority exists.

Use the smallest stage vocabulary: outreach_candidate, research_first, monitor, outside_scope. Research first should identify what public/source/identity ambiguity merits resolution before a conversation; monitor needs an observable future catalyst; outside scope needs affirmative sourced mismatch, not failed retrieval. No quotas or score cutoffs. Do not make all C1-C8 positive, named founders, a working-product inspection, exclusive IP rights or completed clinical validation universal pre-intro gates.

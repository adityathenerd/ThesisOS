## Observable finish line

A genuine matcher result explains why this, why them and why now, and whether to pursue a useful next action now or monitor for a specific trigger. Complete means a complete, inspectable case artifact for the available research: sources, identities, relationships, relevant chronology, interpretations and unresolved gaps are all represented. ## Decision meaning

Pursue means there is a specific useful next action now: targeted investigation or human partner review, with evidence and a decision consequence. Monitor means preserve the case with a concrete future signal, remaining uncertainty and reason not to spend further effort now. Neither means human-approved qualification or sent outreach. Underlying case judgment and operational enums stay compatible; the UI may explain next-action priority separately from monitor/needs_evidence state. Do not invent a new public status or map a proposal straight to qualified.

The existing human decision remains binding: agents recommend; a human approves qualification after evidence gates. For this milestone, completing the qualification-review/outreach pipeline is not a prerequisite to proving one research case with a clear pursue/monitor recommendation. Known YC backing excludes a case from novel-YC counts; unknown backing stays unknown. No claim that this one result is novel to all of YC.

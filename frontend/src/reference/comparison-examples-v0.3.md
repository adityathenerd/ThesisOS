## 1. AlloDirect — workflow specificity warrants investigation

**Observed source claims:** A company release dated 25 September 2026 announces pre-seed financing, describes tissue-ordering software serving surgical facilities, reports production orders since September 2025, identifies Rhett Barker as CEO and James A. Casavant as CTO, and describes Barker's orthopedic sales background. [AlloDirect announcement](https://www.allo.direct/news/allodirect-pre-seed-round-m25).

**Comparison:** C1 is supported as a specific problem claim: ordering tissue across suppliers. C2 has relevant operator-experience signals, but CEO/CTO titles do not by themselves establish founder status or individual product contribution. C3 has an articulated workflow mechanism. C4 has first-party production claims rather than an independently inspected application. C5, measured improvement against existing procurement, remains unknown. C6 has identifiable facility buyers but unverified sales friction. C7 and C8 require investigation of software repeatability, service labor, and the next milestone.

**Product gate:** Partly evidenced; a release describing production is not equivalent to this research having inspected a working workflow. The attempted product-page fetch returned an error. No private application access was attempted.

**Recommendation:** Priority investigate; not yet partner-review ready under the proposed gate. Retrieve an attributable current workflow demonstration and evidence of actual repeat ordering and manual work required. Verify the relevant founder role and current financing/YC status. If the product demonstrably replaces repetitive work and has repeat usage, the review case strengthens. If most value is bespoke manual brokerage without a reusable core, fit weakens.

**Why now:** The recent financing is a verified announcement worth rechecking. The release's regulatory/payment catalyst was not independently checked and must not be repeated as established fact.

## 2. MORF-Bio — tool repeatability is the decisive comparison

**Observed source claims:** A release dated 6 October 2025 announces pre-seed financing, describes microbial bioinformatics/bioprocess software, and reports paying customers. This is historical funding and customer evidence, not confirmation of the current round or revenue. [MORF-Bio announcement](https://morf-bio.com/morf-bio-announces-successful-pre-seed-funding-round/).

The live team page identifies Joyce Bennett as CEO, Gavin Thomas as CSO, and Vicki Springthorpe as CTO and the tool's lead developer/creator. These are company-attributed roles; founder status and commercial rights require separate confirmation. [MORF-Bio team](https://morf-bio.com/meet-the-team/).

**Comparison:** C1 is supported as a specific research-tool use case. C2 has more explicit product-contribution attribution than a generic academic affiliation. C3 is articulated but the magnitude of advantage is unknown. C4 has product/customer claims; C5 needs a worked task comparison. C6 has first-party historic paying-customer evidence but current retention and purchasing context are unknown. C7 needs separation of reusable software from consultancy. C8 needs a current roadmap tied to a decisive product or research milestone.

**Product gate:** Partly evidenced. A worked tutorial linked from the site could not be fetched; no functionality was tested. The site links a newer funding update that also could not be fetched. Current stage is therefore unresolved rather than assumed pre-seed from the older announcement.

**Recommendation:** Priority investigate. Inspect a current worked example using representative data, establish whether scientists can repeat the task without bespoke support, and compare it with their actual alternative. Recheck financing and YC status. Repeatable user value strengthens fit; essentially bespoke analysis with no reusable core weakens it.

**Why now:** No current catalyst is established from the accessible sources. Do not present a 2025 round as fresh in October 2026.

## 3. Graph Therapeutics — scientific progress requires different proof

**Observed source claims:** A 9 January 2025 company release announces pre-seed funding and identifies Gregory Vladimer and Robert Sehlke as co-founders, describing an experimental/computational inflammation-and-immunology platform. [Original company release](https://www.graphtherapeutics.com/PR/Graph%20Therapeutics%20round%20announcement%2020250109.pdf).

A 22 June 2026 announcement reports additional investment, more than $10 million total funding including non-dilutive funding, and internal drug programs. The funding total is not a round label and should not be treated as one. [Graph's 2026 funding/program update](https://www.graphtx.com/articles/graph-therapeutics-secures-over-10-million-in-combined-funding-to-advance-therapeutics-programs-in-inflammation-and-immunology).

A 21 September 2026 research summary describes ex vivo disease-model and compound-response work; the linked poster/data were not examined in this pass. This is evidence that the company publicly describes experiments, not independent confirmation of clinical relevance or efficacy. [Graph's research summary](https://www.graphtx.com/articles/exvivo-model-realism-target-discovery-2026). The current team page continues to identify Vladimer and Sehlke as co-founders and lists additional scientific founders. [Graph team](https://www.graphtx.com/team).

**Comparison:** C1 has a disease-area focus but the lead indication/asset selection requires detail. C2 has attributable relevant founder roles. C3 describes an experimental learning mechanism rather than AI branding alone. C4 and C5 need inspection of underlying results, controls, generalizability, and asset linkage. C6 needs a development/partnering plan at the current program stage. C7 requires asset/data rights checks. C8 needs the next program-specific result and its resource requirements.

**Product gate:** Partly evidenced. The public summary contains scientific detail, but source-level experimental validation has not been inspected sufficiently to clear the proposed therapeutic gate.

**Recommendation:** Priority scientific investigation. Retrieve the underlying experimental material and identify the initial therapeutic program, relevant comparator, contrary results, rights, and next decisive milestone. Verify current financing stage and YC status. Route persuasive findings to a scientific reviewer. Unknown revenue is not an adverse signal for this track.

**Why now:** New program funding and a dated research summary justify re-evaluation. Neither establishes patient benefit. A case built solely from the 2025 pre-seed release would be stale.
